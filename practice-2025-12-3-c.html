原卷无英文标题 · 主题：纽约 Eleven Madison Park 成为首家摘得米其林三星的素食餐厅（9 段 · 351 词）

P1New York's Eleven Madison Park has become the first vegan(素食主义的) restaurant to be awarded three Michelin stars.Q46 首句即答案：首家获米其林三星的素食餐厅。

P2The fine dining establishment received its first three-star Michelin rating in 2011 when the menu was famed for its fancy animal-based dishes.

P3However, last year, the restaurant's co-owner and famous chef, Daniel Humm, made the bold decision to remove meat from the menu, citing our unsustainable food system. While cow milk is still served for tea and coffee, the menu is almost 100 percent vegan.Q47 撤肉的理由：citing our unsustainable food system。

P4At the time, Humm acknowledged the move was risky, admitting that "it wasn't clear if guests would come", but called the gamble "a risk worth taking."Q48 「有风险 + 不确定客人来」与「值得一冒」两面并存。

P5"In view of the climate crisis, I didn't want to open the same restaurant," Humm told the Financial Times in an interview. "If we can show the possibilities of eating plant-based food in this setting, it can open a lot of doors" for others to follow.

P6Now, this impressive new accomplishment validates Eleven Madison Park's decision to take meat off the menu and embrace plant-based foods, with Michelin's 2022 New York guide branding it 'a bold vision of luxury dining'.Q49 2022 纽约指南的原话：a bold vision of luxury dining。

P7"We took the jump to transform Eleven Madison Park into a plant-based fine dining restaurant knowing in our hearts this is what we believed in," stated Humm on Instagram. "Last night, we were honored to be awarded three Michelin stars for the 11th year in a row. I am so grateful to the team members who contributed through its nearly 25-year history. We are also grateful to our guests and partners who believed in our vision and encouraged us to push harder."

P8Three Michelin stars is the highest award, given to chefs who are at the peak of their profession. "Their cooking is elevated to an art form and some of their dishes are destined to become classics," reads Michelin's website, in a clear nod to plant-based foods' growing significance in the culinary(烹饪的) world.Q50 授星的本义：对植物性饮食地位上升的明确认可。

P9"I think luxury companies have a real role to play and a responsibility," says Humm. "The more creative we are, the more beautiful and delicious our future will be."

Passage TwoQuestions 51 to 55

原卷无英文标题 · 主题：基因检测到底有多准，哪些结果能信（7 段 · 349 词）

P1With genetic testing becoming increasingly popular, many people are left wondering exactly how accurate it is. Whether you are taking a DNA test to build your extended DNA family tree, or want precise information on inborn(先天的) health conditions, it is important to understand how accurate genetic tests are, and what information we can rely upon.Q51 it is important to understand how accurate...：重要的是了解准确度。

P2How accurate DNA tests are relies greatly upon the kind of test being taken, on the specific question you ask, and on how complex the genetics behind a trait(特征) is. For example, tests for traits that depend on a single gene provide much more reliable results, because you can see whether a disease-causing trait is present.Q52 单基因性状的检测结果可靠得多。

P3Ancestry tests claim to reveal our genetic identities. But saying you are 30 percent East Asian or American hardly reflects your real ancestry.

P4What about using DNA tests to discover distant family members? There are tools to compare one's DNA with others' to find distant relatives based on their genetic identification. These kinds of applications are generally accurate. It's relatively easy to tell whether two DNA samples belong to close relatives. With distant relatives, results become hazier.

P5Genetic health tests claim to be able to detect certain hereditary(遗传的) diseases, or other health conditions. While certain rare diseases can be easily identified, most potential health conditions cannot be identified by genetic testing alone. The large majority of our traits and diseases also depend upon non-genetic factors, such as lifestyle, the environment, and many others.Q53 多数潜在疾病无法「仅靠」基因检测识别。

P6Genetic tests for multi-factorial traits are often very tricky to interpret. Height, for example, depends on hundreds of genes, each contributing a little to the outcome, together with a bunch of environmental factors. A test can look at many genes at once, but it's difficult to predict how they will play together. Then you should also account for non-genetic factors that are not written in the DNA.Q54 难在预测多个基因「放在一起怎么协作」。

P7Tests that offer to find your perfect romantic match and those claiming to predict personality or talents based on your DNA are pure nonsense. At the moment, the scientific bases for these applications are non-existent or incredibly weak.Q55 pure nonsense：作者对这类检测的强烈否定。
